# SYNTŌPOLIS — Digital Island 0.1

## Status

**CANON — initial foundation**

## Definition

SYNTŌPOLIS is not primarily a game and not a decorative virtual city. It is a digital laboratory in which a small society can be inhabited, observed and tested as a coherent living system.

The project translates the philosophy of cooperation into a spatial, social and computational model. Its purpose is to make abstract principles visible: a person can enter the island, meet its inhabitants, observe its institutions and see how decisions change the condition of the whole society.

## Version 0.1 goal

Build a functioning prototype of an island society with:

- territory: 2 × 2 km;
- population: 100 residents;
- one compact central settlement;
- first-person presence;
- essential systems: water, energy, food, housing, work and education;
- residents with roles, needs and daily activity;
- a dashboard showing the state of society;
- time progression and visible consequences of systemic decisions.

## Core principles

1. **Society is a living system.** Residents, infrastructure, knowledge and nature are interdependent.
2. **Cooperation is structural.** It is represented by rules, resource flows and institutions, not only by dialogue or visual symbolism.
3. **Human scale comes first.** Version 0.1 models one comprehensible community rather than an entire civilization.
4. **The world must be inhabitable.** The model is experienced from inside through space, people and everyday life.
5. **Consequences must be visible.** Decisions affect measurable indicators and the physical world.
6. **Nature is part of the system.** The island has limits, regeneration rates and ecological balance.
7. **The prototype remains expandable.** Later versions may add governance experiments, multiplayer participation, AI agents and alternative social scenarios.

## Initial island

The island is approximately four square kilometres. Its geography should naturally explain the settlement:

- a protected bay and accessible coast;
- a freshwater source or catchment system;
- a central habitable valley or plateau;
- higher terrain providing viewpoints and water flow;
- agricultural and forest zones;
- a compact settlement that can be crossed on foot.

The visual identity should suggest a plausible near future: warm, humane, technologically advanced but not sterile.

## Population

The first 100 residents form a complete miniature society. Every resident has at least:

- identity and age group;
- household;
- residence;
- competence or social role;
- current activity;
- basic needs;
- health and energy state;
- relationships or group membership;
- contribution to and dependence on shared systems.

Version 0.1 may simulate most residents through data and routines while rendering only the residents near the player at full fidelity.

## Essential systems

### Water

Collection, storage, purification, consumption and shortage.

### Energy

Generation, storage, distribution, demand and outages.

### Food

Production, storage, preparation, consumption and seasonal resilience.

### Housing

Capacity, condition, comfort, occupancy and maintenance.

### Work

Necessary tasks, competencies, time, production and unmet labour.

### Education

Knowledge, skills, teaching, learning and long-term social capacity.

## Society dashboard

The interface must make the island legible without reducing it to a single score. Initial indicators:

- population and households;
- water reserve;
- energy balance;
- food reserve;
- housing capacity and condition;
- health;
- education and competencies;
- necessary work coverage;
- ecological condition;
- cooperation and social tension.

## Player position

In version 0.1 the player is an observer-participant. The player can:

- enter the island in first person;
- walk through the settlement and landscape;
- inspect buildings and infrastructure;
- meet residents and inspect their current state;
- open the society dashboard;
- advance or observe time;
- activate limited test scenarios.

The player is not an omnipotent city-builder. The long-term design should preserve the agency of residents and the integrity of the social model.

## Definition of done for 0.1

Version 0.1 is complete when a user can enter the island, walk from the coast to the settlement, observe a believable community of 100 residents, inspect the six essential systems, advance simulation time and see understandable social and environmental consequences.

## Outside the scope of 0.1

- a global open world;
- photorealistic final art;
- complex political institutions;
- unrestricted multiplayer;
- blockchain or token economy;
- fully conversational AI for all residents;
- commercial game balance;
- hundreds of unique buildings.

These remain possible future layers, not requirements for the first prototype.
